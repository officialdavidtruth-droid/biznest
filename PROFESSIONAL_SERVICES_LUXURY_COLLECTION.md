# BizNest Professional Services — Luxury Collection

This build adds a dedicated premium storefront system for every Professional Services sub-niche in BizNest.

## Dedicated storefronts

1. Obsidian Atelier — Graphic Design & Print
2. Noir Identity — Brand Strategy
3. Cobalt Bureau — Marketing & Digital
4. Nocturne Studio — Photography
5. Meridian Advisory — Consulting
6. Ledger House — Accounting & Finance
7. Crown & Chambers — Legal
8. Bluebird People — HR & Recruitment
9. Signal Labs — Web & Software
10. Northstar Systems — IT & Technology
11. Forma House — Architecture & Interiors
12. Axis Engineering — Engineering
13. Foundry Build — Construction

Each mode has a different layout language, typography hierarchy, CTA model, information architecture and visual treatment. They are not color-only variations.

## Operational flows supported

- Services/catalog
- Service detail links
- Start a Project / consultation / quote CTA
- Portfolio or project presentation where relevant
- Reviews and trust content
- Niche-specific stats and proof sections
- Mobile-responsive navigation
- Existing BizNest cart/account integration where applicable

## Template selection

The templates are added to the Store Templates gallery and can be created on demand in production if the database has not been seeded yet.

Virtual IDs use:

`__professional__:<template name>`

## Inspiration direction

The collection uses premium editorial, quiet-luxury, dark-luxury, architectural and high-end agency conventions. It is inspired by the visual principles commonly seen in Pinterest/Dribbble luxury references: oversized typography, restrained palettes, asymmetric grids, immersive imagery, generous whitespace, strong editorial hierarchy and invitation-style CTAs. It does not copy any specific website.
